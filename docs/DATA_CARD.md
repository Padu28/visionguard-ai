# Data Card — VisionGuard Synthetic Steel Surfaces

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Synthetic grayscale surface images with pixel-level defect masks |
| **Generator** | `src/visionguard/datasets/synthetic.py` (seeded, deterministic) |
| **Size** | 1 500 images, 96 × 96, from 300 simulated steel sheets (5 crops each) |
| **Task** | Image classification: `normal`, `scratch`, `inclusion`, `crack`, `patch` |
| **Class mix** | 50 % normal · 15 % scratch · 15 % inclusion · 10 % crack · 10 % patch |
| **Licence** | MIT (generated by this repository) |

## How images are made
Each sheet has its own texture parameters (grain direction, roughness, base intensity, illumination
gradient). Defects are rendered on top with known geometry, so every defect image has an exact mask:
* **scratch** — thin straight line, bright or dark, 0.9–1.6 px wide
* **inclusion** — 2–5 dark Gaussian spots
* **crack** — jagged dark poly-line, optionally branched
* **patch** — large low-contrast irregular stain

## Split
By **sheet instance** (60 / 20 / 20). Crops of the same sheet never appear in different splits, which
prevents near-duplicate leakage. `audit()` reports the number of leaked sheets (expected: 0).

## Real datasets
Set `source: folder` in `configs/data.yaml` and place images in `data/raw/<class>/*.png`.
* **NEU Surface Defect Database** — six steel defect classes (classification).
* **MVTec AD** — industrial anomaly detection with masks (better suited to an anomaly-detection task).
* **KolektorSDD / SDD2** — surface defects on commutators.
Follow each dataset's terms; several are non-commercial research only.

## Known limitations
* Synthetic textures are cleaner and more uniform than production imagery.
* Defects are single-class per image; real parts can carry several defects.
* No camera, lens or motion artefacts beyond the controlled corruptions in the robustness study.
